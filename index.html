shorts-bot

A personal tool for the YouTube channel "Chatchawan D."

shorts-bot is a program the channel owner runs on their own computer. Each day it reads technology and AI headlines from news RSS feeds, writes a short Thai-language summary script, generates narration and illustrations, and uploads the result as a private YouTube Short to the owner's channel. The owner reviews every video before publishing it.

The app is not offered to anyone else, and it is not sold or provided as a service to outside users.

It requests YouTube access to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read those videos' statistics (views, likes, comments and average percentage viewed) so that later scripts can improve. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
